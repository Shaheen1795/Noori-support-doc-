# 📱 Noori - Islamic Calendar & Prayer Companion

Noori is an elegant, privacy-focused, and completely ad-free digital Islamic companion. Built from the ground up to elevate your daily routine, Noori brings a beautifully clean design to your Islamic calendar tracking and prayer scheduling without any distractions.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Dynamic Islamic Calendar:** Keep track of the Hijri calendar alongside your standard schedule with absolute clarity.
- 🕋 **Precise Prayer Coordinates:** Get hyper-localized calculation times with quiet, customizable system notifications.
- 🚫 **100% Ad-Free Experience:** No banners, no pop-ups, and no tracking—just pure focus on your spiritual routine.
- 📱 **Modern Home Widgets:** Bring essential tracking directly onto your home screen using minimal visual layouts.
- 🔒 **Privacy First:** Your location data never leaves your device. No cloud tracking, no hidden analytics.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

Noori is built as a cross-platform mobile application utilizing **Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP)** and native UI layers.

- **Shared Core:** Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) for business logic, calendar arithmetic, and location parsing.
- **iOS UI:** Native SwiftUI (`iosApp`) for performance, system widgets, and Fluid layouts.
- **Android UI:** Jetpack Compose (`composeApp`) for material design scaling.
- **Permissions:** Native CoreLocation (iOS) and Android Permissions API handling.

## 🙋‍♂️ Support & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

If you are using Noori and need help, please review the common issues below or contact us directly.

### Q: Why are my prayer times not updating?
**A:** Noori calculates prayer times locally using your GPS coordinates. If your times look blank, go to your phone's **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services**, find **Noori** (or *Noorilos*), and ensure it is set to **"While Using the App"**. 

### Q: Does this app collect my personal data?
**A:** Absolutely not. Noori does not require a registration account, has no external servers, and tracks zero user analytics. All location calculations happen entirely on your device's local memory.
